# Eval results

> **Provisional.** 134 of 134 grade labels (drafted and independently re-checked) and 20 of
> 60 proof labels (drafted and independently re-checked) haven't been reviewed by a person yet (see [README.md](README.md)).
> `draft-checked` means a second pass against the listing and rubric, not a human review. The 40 Pentel smudge pairs
> were labelled by hand in an earlier test. These numbers are a first measurement until that review is done.

`grade@2026-10-27.1` and `proof-labels@2026-10-28.2`. Baseline: grades 85.1%, proof 85.0%.
